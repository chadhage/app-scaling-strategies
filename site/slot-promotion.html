    Timing and evidence

    The examples omit unbounded queue and approval delays. Readiness, staged observation, long-running work and a rollback window dominate real schedules. Drain may overlap traffic migration; retention is a policy decision, not an automated deadline. Changing the promotion target selects a corresponding illustration; example connections do not change its timings or playback speed.

    Capture actual client timestamps and correlation IDs, control-plane responses, release identities, readiness checks, routing observations and in-flight work. Client clocks and service clocks can differ. The demo’s 2-second retention is a synthetic experiment, not a production recommendation.
