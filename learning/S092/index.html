Fail a measured canary, stop expansion and rehearse reversal. Keep the original failed journeys counted even when old recovery succeeds.

One stage, separate results

Every canary, failure, expansion and rollback action below is teaching-trace data. No deployment or resource operation occurs.

Preconditions: eight fixed synthetic clients; ready retained old-v1; one 25% or 50% candidate-v2 cohort. Only the two or four selected clients attempt a transition. Other old-only clients are comparisons, not migration successes.

  1. Start the measured canary, then observe its fixed synthetic journeys.
  2. Attempt expansion. Failure or missing evidence rejects it and stops new candidate admission.
  3. For a stopped canary, rehearse rollback and inspect desired versus observed routing.
  4. Predict the original canary and recovery results, then check your answer.

Default worked result: 4 attempted, 3 successful, 1 interrupted; expansion rejected; confirmed rollback restores old; recovery SUCCESS but canary FAILURE. Recovery's eight checks do not enlarge the transition denominator.
