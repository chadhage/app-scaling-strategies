S075 — Consumer scale-out

Add consumers; detect duplicate owners and duplicate business effects.

Declare, expand, process, verify

Preconditions: six fixed synthetic events, three partitions, one initial consumer. Expansion adds one or two consumers; each partition requires one held epoch-2 lock. No broker or cloud service is contacted.

  1. Choose exclusive locks, redelivery and the effect-key ledger. Add consumers, process, then verify: seven deliveries must produce six effects.
  2. Check your prediction, start a new attempt, and turn the ledger off. The repeated event must fail with two business effects.
  3. Try overlapping owners with the ledger on: deduplication does not repair ownership. Unobserved evidence must remain UNKNOWN unless a known failure already proves FAIL.

Broken fixtures deliberately allow unsafe synthetic attempts to expose faults. A PASS applies only to the selected fixed batch; it is not an exactly-once guarantee. Read the contract and evidence limits.
