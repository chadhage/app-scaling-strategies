S065 — Hot-partition rebalance rehearsal

A-S065 · D-S065 · SC-S065 · frozen Kano S · one portable lesson

Move HOT without losing its route, effects or single owner

HOT begins on shard-old at synthetic owner epoch 7. Relocate it to shard-new at epoch 8 while three fixed phases each attempt one write and one read on HOT and on unrelated COLD. COLD stays on shard-cold.

H0 and C0 are fixture effects. Acknowledged H1, H2 and H3 must survive hot reads. The target snapshot is copied at cutover. A coordinated route follows the replacement owner; this is one synthetic ordering, not an atomic-cutover guarantee for a real system.

Success needs all twelve operation observations, preserved acknowledged effects, a correct hot route, target exposure and exactly one hot owner in every phase. Known failure wins over missing evidence. Configured intent and successful cold requests do not prove a hot move.

1. Choose a bounded relocation plan

Three phases, twelve operations, six retained attempts. Synthetic IDs only; no accounts, endpoints, authentication, timers or random load.

Conditions frozen when the attempt starts

Attempt 6 complete — unknown. Six-attempt limit reached; explicit reset required.

Observe before → cutover → after. Missing observations show unknown, not inferred routes or owners. The model fences the former owner in the after phase even for the overlap fault.
