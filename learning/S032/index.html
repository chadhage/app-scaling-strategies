S032 · Slot-routing cookie transition

Inspect a slot pin, replay its destination, then reassign and verify the client before retiring blue. 100% nominal green allocation does not move a blue-pinned client.

Objective, fixture and limits

Source S032 / D-S032; A-S032 is one frozen S unit in SC-S032. This is about abstract slot-routing cookies, not worker affinity or externalized session storage.

Preconditions: both synthetic slots are healthy and compatible. Four fixed clients: pinned-a and pinned-b start at blue, green-c starts at green, and unpinned new-d uses bucket 75. For new-d only, green is chosen when 75 is less than the nominal green percent. Pins override allocation.

No real cookie is read or written. No timers, randomness, network, accounts or Azure resources. A completed request at blue is not a migration. A green cookie without a complete observed reply is not proof.

Bounds: 24 actions per run; three complete archived runs with counted eviction; 1000 runs per page lifetime. History is in memory only: reloading or closing the page loses it. No persistent storage is used.

Conduct the rehearsal

  1. Replay the cohort at 100%. Inspect the two destinations still pinned to blue; attempt retirement.
  2. Choose pinned-a, select a reply outcome, and reassign it. Repeat for pinned-b.
  3. Observe that missing or interrupted replies block retirement. Replay to resolve current evidence; earlier failed or unknown attempts remain in the run impact.
  4. Retire only after all four clients have fresh completed green evidence and allocation is 100%. Applying allocation invalidates earlier evidence, even when the percentage stays the same.

Whole decimal 0–100; no signs, spaces, leading zeros or exponent notation. Applying allocation never changes a slot pin.

Ready. Replay the cohort to collect observations.
