Expire readiness before promotion; force revalidation instead of trusting an old green snapshot.

Worked exercise

Preconditions: JavaScript enabled, fixed synthetic blue-v1 source and green-v2 candidate. The initial healthy snapshot is from tick 0. An observation is fresh only at age less than 2 ticks. At age 2 it expires. These invented ticks are not a real clock or Azure health threshold.

  1. Choose the next observation and elapsed ticks, then start.
  2. Advance the clock to expire the original green. Attempt promotion: FAILURE safely blocks it.
  3. Revalidate, then attempt promotion again. Healthy → SUCCESS, unhealthy → FAILURE, missing → UNKNOWN.
  4. Alternatively, advance again after revalidation, before promotion: even refreshed green expires.
  5. Predict the latest outcome and retain the whole attempt. New attempt keeps history; reset erases it.

One revalidation, at most two advances, three promotion decisions and seven trace events per attempt. At most six checked attempts; none is silently evicted.
