Recovery after new writes

S098 · A-S098 · frozen satisfier S · LP-COMPARE

Preserve the committed effects, not just old software

Start with fixed reservation L001 = 6 units. Apply v2’s fixed reserve +3 and release −1: all three effects must still read correctly as 8 units after compatible rollback or forward recovery. The old 6-unit snapshot is no longer correct.

This is a memory-only workbook, with no real writes or recovery operations. The payload contracts are fixed; no schema migration is performed. The illustrative read budget is 10 ms; available synthetic reads take 7 ms.

  1. Apply the fixed v2 writes.
  2. Choose a recovery plan and read observation; rehearse the decision.
  3. Predict the result and check your interpretation. A learner PASS is not a successful recovery.
  4. Reset to compare another plan. Reset retains history, failures and denominators.

Operator controls

At most 12 decisions, including safe rejections. Reload deliberately clears all in-memory history.
