Objective and preconditions

Advance internal → pilot → limited → remaining in order. Within a ring, this fixture requires frontend v2 + API v2 + worker v2; mixed versions are explicitly incompatible.

  • Eight fictional members, two per ring. Internal starts observed on v2; all other rings start pending on v1.
  • Deployment lifecycle starts completed: both v1 and v2 tier artifacts are already available in this fixture. Ring rollout status is separate; deployment completion does not authorize expansion.
  • The shared dependency is used by every ring. Its dual contract accepts v1 and v2; v2-only breaks existing v1 consumers before they move.
  • Predecessor observations must be healthy, the promotion window open, and the target observation at least 120 fixture seconds with two journeys within 800 ms each.
  • 120 seconds is a synthetic fixture value, not elapsed time. The exercise runs immediately and collects no production telemetry.

Worked path: keep pilot / all-v2 / dual contract / open / healthy and promote. Reset; choose v2-only to stop before expansion. Reset; choose unknown worker evidence to see why uncertainty cannot unlock limited.
