S017 — Vertical scaling through replacement

Compare a disruptive In-place resize with Replace with larger capacity: prepare a separate larger deployment, observe readiness, then hand off admissions.

A-S017 · D-S017 · SC-S017 · One frozen S unit. Native B-S017 and source-parent acceptance remain separate.

Worked comparison

  1. Keep demand 4 and healthy observations. Choose In-place resize, then observe three windows. Expect 12 attempted, 8 successful, 4 interrupted, 6 synthetic credits, FAIL.
  2. Archive/reset. Choose Replace with larger capacity. Observe two windows: larger capacity is READY, but admissions still use the old deployment.
  3. Hand off, then observe one more window. Expect 12 successful, zero interrupted, 9 synthetic credits and PASS. Compare the retained runs below.
  4. Try demand 6, candidate failure, or missing observations. Recovery must not erase prior impact or uncertainty.

Fixed teaching envelope: old capacity 4, target 8 journeys/window; 5 seconds/window; one restart window versus two observed warm-up windows. Old/new cost 1/2 credits per full window, including restart. Parallel overlap costs 3 credits, including after handoff because old capacity remains retained. No clock, randomness, retries, drain, retirement, real billing or platform behavior is measured.

Conduct the rehearsal

1. Apply demand before starting Blank, fractions, exponent notation and unknown fields are refused, never coerced.
2. Choose one strategy
3. Observe, then hand off only when ready
