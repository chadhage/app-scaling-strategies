S072 — Cold-cache refill comparison

Compare unbounded and coalesced refill; identify a database-overloading stampede.

A-S072 · D-S072 · SC-S072 · Kano S · Standalone, synthetic only

Prepare and predict

All requests arrive at virtual time 0 after a cold-cache cutover. Keys are assigned round-robin. The synthetic database accepts only its declared concurrent capacity. Excess refills fail immediately; there are no retries, real timers, network calls, database connections, or live cache operations.

  • Unbounded: every request starts a refill, even for the same key.
  • Coalesced: requests for a key share one refill; different keys still start together.
  • Bounded + coalesced: distinct-key refills queue FIFO with at most the chosen limit active. The limit must fit database capacity. Queue time counts against the original journey budget.

Start with defaults: unbounded should yield 2 successes and 6 overload failures. Switch only strategy to coalesced: 1 refill should serve all 8 journeys in 40 ms. The earlier 6 failures must remain counted.

Try the failure and uncertainty limits

For multi-key pressure choose 8 requests and 4 keys. Coalescing alone attempts 4 refills against capacity 2: 4 journeys fail. Then choose bounded + coalesced, limit 1, and budget 80 ms: 0 overloads but 4 latency failures, with completion times 40, 80, 120, and 160 ms. A limit of 2 meets that 80 ms budget in this model.

Withhold one otherwise-successful observation to produce an unknown, never a success. Known overload and deadline failures are not hidden by missing evidence. A reported maximum is only the maximum observed latency, not a claim that every journey completed.

Limits: one isolated burst, fixed-duration refills, fixed capacity, FIFO order, identical synthetic values per key, no background load, partial completion, retry, TTL, multi-process lock, or real concurrent cache/database behavior. This is not a native B-S072 experiment, completed D-S072 parent, production Node.js 24 validation, or a statistical/five-nines availability result.
