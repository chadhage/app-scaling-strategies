S039 — Instance-local temporary state

Move ownership, not just traffic: preserve required uploads, workflow state and generated artifacts.

A-S039 · D-S039 · SC-S039 · one satisfier, not a completed native source demo.

Conduct the rehearsal

  1. Choose an artifact and its storage. Prepare on A seeds fixed ASCII bytes and a required workflow checkpoint.
  2. Handoff to B changes the serving owner. It does not copy A's local state. B starts empty.
  3. Verify on B compares the retrieved bytes, byte count, teaching checksum and checkpoint with the retained original.
  4. Compare durable/intact success with local/intact loss. Then try empty 200, corruption, missing checkpoint and unavailable evidence.

“Durable” means a simulated shared in-memory store outside the two worker stores, not real persistent storage. No account, server or credentials are required. No authentication or storage durability guarantee is modeled.

Fixed synthetic conditions (locked after preparation)

Use Tab / Shift+Tab, native arrow keys, Enter or Space. Reset restores defaults and artifact focus, not history or budgets. At most 12 runs and 12 verified journeys per page; no timers or unbounded work.
