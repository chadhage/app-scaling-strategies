Priming all four phases gives 40 + 20 + 10 + 20 + 10 = 100 ms. These are deterministic teaching constants, not stopwatch measurements or percentiles.

  3. Keep phases primed but set an 80 ms budget: initialization succeeds, latency fails; HOLD.
  4. Try failed/unmeasured phases or an unmeasured/unsupported route. Missing timing is UNKNOWN, not zero.
