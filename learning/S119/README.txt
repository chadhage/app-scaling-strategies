   committed sequence 240; Amber's shallow restore alone authorizes no return.
2. Rehearse return at 25% before either data/ownership check. Observe NEGATIVE,
   effective 0%, zero attempted migrations, and a retained safe abort.
3. Inspect synchronization DATA: 240 == 240, both contract-v4. Inspect
   ownership DATA: sole Amber writer, epoch 8, old Cobalt writer fenced.
   These are fixed assertions, not synchronization or ownership operations.
4. Return at 25, 50, then 100. Capacity requirements are 5, 10, 20 units.
   Each cohort's two records must show actual intended fictional Amber use,
   unchanged identity/session/cart, visible committed effects exactly once,
   and completion within the original 400 ms journey budget.
5. Inspect six passed synthetic journeys and completed synthetic return.
   A gate-only success, old-origin-only journey or unknown observation does
   not establish migration.
6. Reset and try lagging-state, unfenced-writer, or capacity-shortfall.
   Observe failed gates, no exposure, no successful completed failback.
7. Try continuity-break or latency-breach. The 25% cohort passes; at 50% a
   duplicate effect/lost state or 620 ms latency fails. Four attempted
   journeys remain: three passed, one failed. Further return is held, not
   retrospectively turned into success by rollback.
8. Try restore-unknown, sync-unknown, ownership-unknown, routing-unknown or
   journey-unknown. Missing observations remain UNKNOWN. Routing-mismatch
   leaves the measured effective route at Cobalt and fails both journeys.
