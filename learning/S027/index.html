S027 — False-positive health response

Reject an error, redirect, or misleading 200 before candidate eligibility. A healthy-looking response is not proof of a working business path.

Card A-S027 · parent D-S027 · packet SC-S027 · one frozen S unit

The contract, not just “HTTP 200”

Only the original status 200 AND a matching business payload permit eligibility. The payload must have ready: true, candidate: "candidate-v2" and profileId: "training-profile". Redirects are not followed; absent evidence is unknown, never success.

  1. Choose 500, redirect, or misleading 200. Predict HOLD, then check.
  2. For misleading 200, predict ADMIT. The wrong answer fails; the gate still holds.
  3. Compare the valid 200 control and the 200 with unobserved business evidence.

Preconditions: one fixed candidate, isolated synthetic profile, two assertions per check. No server, subscription, sign-in or setup is needed. No real network probe occurs.
