S048: Batch job retirement

A-S048 · D-S048 · one frozen S unit · packet SC-S048

Objective: finish a batch before retiring its host, or retain a usable checkpoint and resume correctly. A checkpoint is not a completed job.

Fixed worked exercise

Six synthetic items each commit one effect of 10. Every processing attempt costs 10 synthetic ms. Start commits items 1–3 on the old worker. Retire that active worker, then continue or verify. Correct completion requires all six items, exactly one effect each (total 60), within the original job budget.

Defaults: durable cursor 3, idempotent effects, 20 ms resume delay, original budget 100 ms. Expect a resumable job at retirement, then correct completion at 80 ms. A stale cursor 1 replays items 2–3: compare idempotency against duplicate effects. Try falsely claiming completion after item 3.

Preconditions: local browser HTML/JS only. No account, credentials, subscription, server or installation. All time, checkpoints, effects and worker identities are synthetic; no Azure or batch-runtime calls occur.

1. Choose assumptions and act

Assumptions lock at Start. The budget starts before item 1, not at replacement. Checkpoint, replay and delay inputs are unused when finishing before retirement. Forced retirement and false completion do not resume.

Limits

This finite model is not durable storage, a live batch service, a native retirement protocol, a production Node.js 24 certification, or proof of exactly-once delivery. B-S048 and D-S048 require separate native evidence. No identities, tokens, keys or authentication are modeled.

Unknown effects are never counted as zero or successful. Known loss, false completion or a budget breach still fails when other proof is unknown. Timing is deterministic arithmetic, not measured browser or Azure latency.
