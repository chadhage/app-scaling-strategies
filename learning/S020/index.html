Protect existing service when expansion is refused

Exhaust a declared harness allowance, request unsafe expansion, and attempt promotion. The requested target must not consume the protected reserve or receive traffic after rejection. Probe existing journeys rather than inferring continuity from a blocked button or HTTP 200.

Fixture preconditions: two reserved units already serve four existing journeys per probe. Each unit represents two journeys per synthetic window. The allowance includes the reserve. Admitted sandbox units are held off-route; allocation is not evidence of candidate readiness.
