S050: Retire only after accounting for pinned clients

A-S050 · D-S050 · SC-S050 · one frozen S unit

Moving fresh arrivals does not clear an old keep-alive connection, a routing-cookie pin, or already-admitted work. Keep the old destination usable until each required dependency is resolved.

Conduct a bounded rehearsal

Four fixed synthetic client checks begin when you route new arrivals: fresh-1, keepalive-1, pinned-1, and work-1. Each contributes once to the denominator. No real connection or cookie is created. “Finish” without a verified final work receipt is unknown.

  1. Choose how to handle each old dependency; route fresh arrivals to the candidate.
  2. Resolve according to your choices; inspect what still requires the old destination.
  3. Request retirement. Guarded retirement blocks known or unverified dependencies. Forced retirement is an explicit negative experiment, not a bypass that can claim success.

Preconditions and limits

Use a modern browser with local HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No account, server, credentials, infrastructure, actual authentication, real cookies or network requests are needed. The fixture has four clients, three dependencies, three steps, and at most six retained attempts; there is no timer, load generator, or random outcome.

This portable model does not prove TCP keep-alive behavior, routing-cookie behavior, native Azure retirement, production Node.js 24 support, five-nines reliability, or completion of B-S050 or D-S050. No cloud, IaC, scaffold, registration, deployment, or spend is authorized.
