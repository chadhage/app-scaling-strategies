Manual horizontal scale-out

Add instances, evidence effective ready capacity, and only then increase their traffic share. A requested instance, a deployed instance, a ready instance, and a traffic-eligible instance are not interchangeable.

Objective, preconditions, and worked exercise

Source: README.md S011 and .docs/wiki/backlog.md D-S011. The original D-S021 readiness prerequisite and the separate B-S011 native proof are still incomplete.

The fixture starts with two ready, eligible instances at 80 requests/second. All values below are declared teaching assumptions, not Azure limits or measurements. Slots share this fixture's compute pool; adding a slot does not add an instance.

  • 50 requests/second per instance; an 80% utilization ceiling per old pool, new pool, and total ready pool.
  • 20 pooled dependency connections per deployed instance, including warming instances; default aggregate limit 100.
  • 800 ms complete-journey budget; preserved required connection, intact state, and exactly one synthetic business effect.
  • Readiness is valid for at most 30 logical seconds. Each action advances 10 seconds; the age action advances 31. No wall-clock observation or timer is implied.
  • 20 deterministic client records per admission sample. Only clients admitted to added instances enter the transition denominator; old-only journeys stay separate.
  1. Keep the default four instances, 160 requests/second, 100 connections, and healthy fixture. Request scale-out: counts are 4 / 2 / 2 / 2.
  2. Observe twice: first 4 / 4 / 2 / 2, then 4 / 4 / 4 / 2. Verify fresh effective capacity; eligible remains two.
  3. Increase added-instance share to 50%: eligible becomes four; inspect actual client instance IDs and budget assertions.
  4. Reset, select a fault, and compare. Recovering a fixture allows another sample but never rewrites failed or unknown history.

Conduct the rehearsal

1. Freeze a bounded synthetic request

The choice is frozen for this request. Recovery selects healthy observations in a new epoch while retaining the original choice and every earlier result.

2. Observe and verify before exposure
3. Increase admission and inspect actual journeys

The 20-client sample resolves shares in 5-point increments, rounded down. Both requested and observed pool loads must meet their ceilings; a sample with no added-instance client is blocked.

Recovery is a deterministic fixture restart, not a native rollback, drain, or repair of already interrupted users. Reset clears only this page's in-memory run.
