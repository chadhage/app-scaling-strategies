Training rehearsal - synthetic data - not a live Azure result

Identify the check that must prevent deletion when required v1 dependencies remain. “100% routed to v2”, “healthy v2” and green requests are not retirement evidence.

Conduct → inspect → select → check

  1. Begin Outstanding dependencies and conduct Verify retirement.
  2. Inspect every required inventory and guard. A known blocker or missing observation must hold retirement.
  3. Select an exact guard, its dependency and the recorded decision, then check your answer.
  4. Optionally record the two named fictional handoff and retention-end observations and verify again. The same original journey, original budget and earlier interruption remain recorded.
  5. Archive to compare another bounded snapshot. Inspect or copy the read-only synthetic report; reset explicitly to clear it.

All versions, sessions, jobs, in-flight work, effects, leases, assets, contracts, rollback clocks and retirement labels are fixed teaching DATA ONLY. No deletion, file removal, cloud or user-data operation exists.

Bounds: four retained rehearsals, five checks of retirement per rehearsal, twelve trace events and two fictional retention ticks. Reload starts fresh memory; there is no persistence or server.
