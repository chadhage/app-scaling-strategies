One green probe is not a stable window

Compare an unsafe single-green rule with a fresh consecutive readiness window. No management calls, live probes, credentials or Azure resources are involved.

Worked exercise

Preconditions: healthy GA and a compatible candidate are assumed, not measured. Six synthetic probes occur one tick apart. No new probe arrives after tick 6.

  1. Check the default flapping sequence at tick 6: two recent greens still follow a failed probe.
  2. Select Recovered and predict ALLOW: three fresh consecutive greens can pass.
  3. Inspect at tick 9: old greens become unknown, not current readiness.

Every selected probe must be successful and no older than the maximum age (inclusive). A failed fresh probe blocks admission; missing, not-yet-observed or stale evidence is unknown. The window is a count of consecutive one-tick probes, spanning count minus one ticks. Thresholds are lesson choices, not Azure recommendations.

Operator controls

S = success; F = failure; ? = unknown. Tab, arrow keys and Enter operate these native controls. Changing controls clears only the current result. History holds at most eight checked runs in memory.
