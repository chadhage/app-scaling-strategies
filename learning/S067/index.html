The contract before the route

During a migration, reader capacity can exist while its data is too old. A selected route is not proof of a fresh returned value.

One fixed synthetic primary and reader hold SYN-RECORD-067. The primary is at committed version 12, tick 12. One version is committed at each synthetic tick. These ticks are not elapsed milliseconds or a measured database lag.

  1. Declare acceptable staleness and introduce the supplied reader lag.
  2. Read once, inspect the selected reader and the returned version against the minimum.
  3. Check your prediction. Try a gated route in a new attempt and compare retained history.

Defaults deliberately fail: reader version 7 does not meet minimum 12. A freshness gate can select primary without repairing or advancing reader-1. A two-tick budget accepts version 10 exactly. Unknown or absent evidence cannot pass.

Evidence boundary and source

All lag, watermarks, versions, routes and results here are synthetic. There are no database connections, management calls, authentication or credentials. The primary is assumed current; this model does not certify replication, read-your-writes, elapsed-time freshness or a production database.

S067 in the source README requires routing consistency-sensitive reads appropriately rather than silently returning unacceptable stale data. D-S067 additionally requires actual lag and reader observations. This rehearsal does not satisfy those native gates.

This is controlled lag during migration, not S061 replica expansion. B-S067 and D-S067 remain incomplete. No native/source-parent, production Node.js 24, unattended lifecycle or availability certification is implied.
