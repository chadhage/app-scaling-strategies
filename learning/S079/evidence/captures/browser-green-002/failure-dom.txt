S079 — Backlog surge

Add processing capacity without sacrificing per-key ordering or completion deadlines. Throughput alone is not success.

A-S079 · frozen S · D-S079 · packet SC-S079 · resource-free synthetic mode

Declared contract

Preconditions: JavaScript enabled; two independent order keys A and B; one initial worker. All jobs arrive at synthetic tick 0. First job per key takes 3 ticks; later jobs take 1. No random values or wall-clock timing.

Ordering: each key must commit sequence 1, 2, 3… in that order. There is no global A/B ordering requirement. Same-tick completions commit in ascending worker-number order.

Deadline: every job must complete at or before the declared tick, measured from burst arrival, not from its worker start. Eventual completion after that tick is a failure.

Bounds: 6 or 12 jobs, 1–3 total workers, at most 16 synthetic ticks and 6 retained attempts. No jobs are silently evicted from history.

README S079: “Backlog surge: Add processing capacity while maintaining ordering and completion-deadline requirements.” D-S079 requires real work to drain within its declared deadline and ordering contract; overdue jobs are failures. Its original dependency is D-S075.

This portable A-card models that decision only.
