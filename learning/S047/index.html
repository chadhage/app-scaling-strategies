S047: Continue the application, not the original gRPC stream

Infrastructure closes the original stream at cutover. Use the application's last acknowledged result to request the next result on a new stream. Compare a synthetic native-shaped gRPC receipt with a separate browser-proxy observation.

Neither observation is a real protocol exchange. Ordinary HTTP 200, successful proxy output, and this lesson cannot prove native HTTP/2/gRPC continuation.

Conduct the bounded exercise

  1. Choose how many results v1 acknowledged, a continuation policy, and the two evidence conditions.
  2. Start v1, then Cut over while its stream is active.
  3. Resume on v2, then Check result. Compare protocol/trailers, sequences, and the original budget.
  4. Try ordinary HTTP, missing receipts, a dropped or repeated result, and a short budget. Reset retains outcomes, including unfinished attempts as UNKNOWN.

Fixture: eight results numbered 1–8; 10 synthetic milliseconds per emitted result. The completion budget begins before cutover and is inclusive. These are lesson parameters, not Azure limits. No server, account, credentials, framework, or cloud access is needed.
