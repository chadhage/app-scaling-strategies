S015 — Horizontal scale-in

A-S015 · D-S015 · frozen S · SC-S015. Preserve admitted work before removing capacity.

Objective, preconditions, and limits

Use host-supported admission, drain, or continuation mechanisms to preserve work before capacity terminates. Here, retiring-A owns one admitted request and one job at tick 0. retained-B is ready and has room for this entire bounded fixture; no real host is contacted.

Default request: 2 ticks. Job: 4 ticks. Original work budget: 6 ticks from admission. Drain budget: 5 ticks from the closure action. Equality at a deadline is allowed. A checkpoint changes job ownership, not its original deadline. A request never relocates.

  1. Start the fixture, close new admission, and probe fresh admission.
  2. Drain: advance 4 ticks. Continuation: checkpoint the job, then advance 2 ticks.
  3. Request safe retirement; inspect actual capacity, not just the request.
  4. Probe after retirement. For continuation, advance 2 more ticks to finish the job. Verify.
  5. Reset to archive the attempt. Try missing evidence, stale routing, short budgets, or the explicit unsafe fault.

The verifier, admitted-work outcome, and synthetic cleanup are separate. Passing a negative-test assertion does not turn lost work into continuity. Probes measure routing only; neither probes nor these two work items prove user migration, Azure support, or production reliability.
