S089 — Control-plane outage

Lose management connectivity; separate control-plane availability from data-plane service.

All connectivity, commands, routes and recovery below are fixed synthetic trace data, not network or resource operations. Established routes should not require the controller for every request. The shared training harness is a separate reporting/recovery path.

Before you rehearse

  • JavaScript enabled; no server, account, CLI or cloud connection required.
  • Established blue-v1 route, synthetic epoch 7; no migration or routing change occurs.
  • Three fixed established-user samples per attempt; budget 100 synthetic milliseconds.
  • The fault affects only the per-run management path, never the shared harness.
  • Four fixtures × two recovery observations; six events and at most six retained attempts.
