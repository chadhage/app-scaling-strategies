S014: Schedule demand, then prove capacity was ready

Provision and warm capacity before a known spike. A correct calendar is a plan, not a readiness observation or a client result.

Conduct the rehearsal

Objective: observe a supported synthetic schedule firing, additional units deploying and warming strictly before demand, and complete client journeys meeting their budget.

Preconditions: two ready units; a declared 20-second demand window; synthetic observations only. No account, server, framework, or management access is needed.

  1. Choose demand time, lead, target capacity, provisioning and warm-up durations, and an observation fixture.
  2. Activate Schedule demand spike. Compare the calendar with the observed deployed and ready counts.
  3. Advance the logical clock in 5-second steps until the complete window ends, then Check outcome.
  4. Try lead time 5: the same correct calendar produces readiness at second 40, after demand begins at 30. Keep that failure, then conduct a healthy attempt.

Fixture contract: each ready unit supports 40 requests/s; 10 journeys are sampled every 5 seconds at demand start and three subsequent times. All 40 must complete correctly within 800 ms. The clock is logical, not real time. These are teaching values, not Azure limits.
