The precise source objective is to mix producer/consumer schemas and choose
compatibility checks BEFORE introducing a new producer. Four independent test
journeys cover old->old, old->new, new->old and new->new. Each starts with its own
empty hypothetical consumer ledger; this is not broker fan-out or double credit.
Old events intend 1250 cents and new events intend 2500 cents. Their 7500-cent
test sum is not an account balance. The fixed account/event names are synthetic.

Worked exercise:
1. Keep additive / tolerant / all contracts AND effects / complete. Inspect all
   four checks, then request introduction: success, 4 correct effects, 7500 cents.
2. Try renamed: the new consumer succeeds, but the old consumer cannot read the
   new field. Failure blocks expansion before the producer is introduced.
3. Try unit-drift with full checks: all shapes are interpretable, but the two new
   event journeys credit 25 rather than 2500 cents. Failure, observed sum 2550.
4. Choose shape-only, new-only or withhold-old: absent effect/old-reader evidence
   is UNKNOWN, never success. Strict old readers also reject additive fields.
