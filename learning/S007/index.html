Move new sessions.
Finish old journeys.

A new admission boundary is not a request-weight change. Retain blue until observed old work finishes, pinned clients are resolved, and rollback retention ends.

Objective & fixture preconditions

Move NEW sessions to ready green while the already admitted blue cart completes with its original subject and valid state. Then explain why zero active journeys, 100% nominal weight, or an expired timer alone is insufficient to retire blue.

  • old-journey: active on blue; schema 1, subject learner-old, cart item-A, revision 1. Its one finish request must produce revision 2 and one receipt.
  • old-pinned: idle, but still bound to blue with its own item-P cart. It remains a dependency even when no request is currently running.
  • old-work: one pending blue effect. Completion must record exactly one effect-old-work, not fabricate a request or replay lost work.
  • Green starts unassessed. The readiness check represents a warm, compatible schema-1 fixture, not a real health probe or session-store migration.
  • Rollback retention lasts ten synthetic clock minutes after cutover. Advancing that clock does not finish work. No real-time observation is implied.

Worked exercise

  1. Apply Switch NEW admission to green before readiness: expect rejection. Apply Check green readiness with the compatible fixture, then switch. Old bindings must not change.
  2. Apply Record nominal green weight at 100%. This is advisory only. Apply Admit a NEW session with new-green-001, then Read through old pinned client. The actual request table must show green and blue, regardless of the displayed weight.
  3. Try Guarded retirement: reject. Finish the admitted old journey, reconnect the idle pinned client at a new green session boundary, and finish the blue work. Inspect the cart, receipt, and one effect.
  4. Drain is now complete, but rollback retention is still open. Guarded retirement must still reject. Advance the clock by ten minutes, then retire blue. Green remains available.
  5. Counterexample: Reset; check compatible green and switch admission. Select UNSAFE: force blue retirement, then read the old pinned client. Observe interrupted sessions, lost work, and an unavailable request. The safety verdict is FAIL, not a successful migration.
Two additional experiments

Reset; check compatible green; record 100% nominal green weight without switching; admit a session. Its observed start still reaches blue. Finish that extra blue session by its ID before retirement.

During retention, admit a green session and roll back NEW admission to blue. Finish the admitted green session by its ID: it stays on green. Rollback does not teleport or kill already admitted journeys. This exercise offers rollback only within the declared retention window.
