S030 — Warm-up overload

Limit initialization concurrency so candidate preparation does not overwhelm shared dependencies.

One plan, two preparation strategies

Compare starting every candidate at once with a bounded batch size. Existing GA journeys and initialization share a database and cache. No deployment, network call or traffic switch occurs.

  • Each candidate takes exactly two synthetic ticks to initialize, using 2 database + 1 cache units per tick. Batches do not overlap; failed initialization is not retried.
  • Four GA journeys arrive per tick, using 4 database + 4 cache units in total. Overload means demand is strictly greater than capacity.
  • An overloaded tick fails all four journeys: 250 ms instead of 50 ms, against a 100 ms budget. Candidates in that batch fail initialization at its completion boundary.
  • SUCCESS requires every candidate initialized, every journey within budget, and preparation within its tick budget. Missing observations produce UNKNOWN, not a pass.

Worked exercise: six candidates with bound two, database eight, cache six and budget eight take six ticks safely; simultaneous preparation takes two ticks but overloads both dependencies. A bound of one protects journeys yet takes twelve ticks and misses the preparation budget.

Preconditions: isolated compatible candidates, a fixed four-journey baseline and sufficient GA capacity before preparation. These are fixture assumptions, not established Azure behavior. Different durations have different journey denominators; do not compare raw success counts as equal exposure.
