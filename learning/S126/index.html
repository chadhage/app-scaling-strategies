Read before conducting

  • ACA multiple-revision mode allows weighted traffic and manual revision activation/deactivation. Revisions are not App Service slots; a software update is not proof of added compute.

  • Two canary and three promotion journeys use a fixed 400 ms original budget. These small samples cannot establish native support, real-user migration or five-nines reliability.
