Training rehearsal - synthetic data - not a live Azure result

Objective
Swap settings with distinct identities/networks; identify properties that do
not move. The authored fixture exchanges only DISPLAY_LABEL. DATA_ALIAS,
principal and network tokens stay with each fictional destination. The separate
permission/path observations concern that effective destination, not the sender.

Exercise
1. Choose one of four fixed fictional access observations and a prediction.
2. Rehearse the fictional exchange. Inspect the before/after table; access is
   still unknown until explicitly observed.
3. Inspect the destination data. Compare the prediction, lesson outcome and
   separate permission/path result. An incorrect prediction fails visibly even
   if access happens to pass. Destination denial/blockage is negative; missing
   observations stay unknown. Neither pending nor unknown counts as positive.
4. Repeat with other fixtures. At most six independent rehearsals/twelve events
   remain in memory. All attempts, including failures and unknowns, remain.
5. Reset explicitly to clear history, restore selections/table and focus.
   Reload also clears this in-memory lesson. Keyboard operation is supported.

slot-specific properties at the effective destination. D-S105 specifically
requires REAL slot-specific settings/access after transition and visible failure

fixed fictional teaching DATA ONLY. The movement rule is not an authoritative
