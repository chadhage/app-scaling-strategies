STATE AND HISTORY
An eligible initial admission records one active admitted release and three
C023 modeled applications. An expansion revalidates both tiers for that same
release/profile, changes its stage once, and preserves the original complete job
without applying effects again. Candidate source stamps describe the fresh
compatibility check; the retained job keeps its original execution lineage.
Rejected transitions retain the original active job and all admission/effect
counts; rejection history grows instead of rewriting an earlier attempt.
Inspections add no admission/effect. Stale expectedRevision and reused request
identities refuse transitions. Reset archives the active job and preserves all
operation history, inspections, rejections and lifetime effect counts. A retained
release/work identity cannot be reused after reset.

The private, frozen operation journal is replayed and compared with the private
projection before reads/dispatch and after append. Replay regenerates only pure
local model values; it does not perform external business effects. Returned
snapshots are detached, recursively frozen, inspection/export only. They are NOT
authentication or trusted receipts and can never be adopted as current state.
This is private in-memory session integrity, not an authorization boundary
against hostile scripts or proxies controlling the same JavaScript realm.

Bounds: 16,384 candidate characters, 128 characters per selector, 64 operations,
bounded own-data input shape and depth. Accessors are rejected without invocation,
as are custom prototypes, symbols, cycles, sparse arrays, nonfinite/coercive
values, extra/missing fields and duplicate JSON keys. One separate immutable
terminal HISTORY_LIMIT refusal is retained after operation 64; further commands,
including reset, cannot truncate or bypass the history bound.

BROWSER CONDUCT
