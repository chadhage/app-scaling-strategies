account, framework, or foreground-browser lease is needed.

1. Tab through **Historical failure fixture** to **Inspect historical failure**;
   press Enter. Read the exact C015 error code, explicit pending run/epoch/step,

   **Stop rehearsal**. Reinspection is at most three same-observation teaching
